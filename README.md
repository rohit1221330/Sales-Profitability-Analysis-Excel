# 📊 Sales & Profitability Analysis — Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a **750-row sales dataset** using **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product performance, and customer/channel trends.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into a structured analytical model and extract **actionable business insights** using Excel's data analysis, reporting, and visualization features.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. 💻 Laptop was the strongest revenue contributor

Laptop generated approximately **₹1.05 Cr in revenue**, making it the highest-revenue product in the dataset.

This indicates that laptops were a major contributor to overall sales and should remain an important product category for revenue planning.

---

### 2. 📍 Highest Revenue Does Not Necessarily Mean Highest Profitability

The analysis highlighted differences between **revenue performance and profitability** across regions.

**Chennai generated the highest total profit**, while **Hyderabad recorded the lowest regional profit margin at 7.42%**.

This demonstrates why business performance should be evaluated using both revenue and profitability metrics.

---

### 3. 💰 Revenue Alone Is Not Enough

The business generated:

* **₹2.79 Cr in Net Sales**
* **₹23.95 Lakh in Profit**
* **8.59% Overall Profit Margin**

The relatively low margin compared with total sales highlights the importance of monitoring costs, discounts, and transaction-level profitability.

---

### 4. ⚠️ Loss-Making Transactions Require Attention

Loss-making orders were identified using calculated profit logic and **COUNTIFS**.

These transactions can negatively affect overall profitability and should be investigated based on factors such as:

* Discount levels
* Product cost
* Sales channel
* Region
* Customer type

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve profitability:

1. **Monitor discount levels** on low-margin transactions.
2. Investigate the causes behind **Hyderabad's lower profit margin**.
3. Continue focusing on high-performing products such as **Laptops** while monitoring their margins.
4. Identify recurring patterns in **loss-making transactions**.
5. Evaluate profitability by **region, channel, product, and customer type**, rather than relying only on revenue.
6. Use monthly profit trends to support better **sales and financial planning**.
